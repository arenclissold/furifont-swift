# Bundled Furifont resources

These unmodified resources come from Furifont v0.3.0 by Anthony Fu:
https://github.com/antfu/furifont/releases/tag/v0.3.0

- `FurifontSerifVF.ttf`: Furifont Serif VF, derived from Adobe Source Han Serif,
  licensed under SIL OFL 1.1. The full font licence is in `OFL.txt`.
- `FurifontSerifVF.ivs.json`: matching reading order from the release.
- `okurigana.json`: matching packed data from
  https://github.com/antfu/furifont/blob/v0.3.0/packages/dictionary/data/okurigana.json
